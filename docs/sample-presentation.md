# Enterprise AI Document Intelligence

## Executive overview

AI Digest transforms documents into evidence-backed enterprise analysis. Users upload Markdown, text, HTML, JSON or XML documents and receive executive summaries, risks, findings, recommendations and source references.

## Business problem

- Manual document review is slow and inconsistent.
- Technical, compliance and business risks are difficult to correlate.
- Evidence and source references are often lost across reports.

## Solution architecture

- Angular enterprise dashboard.
- .NET 10 Web API.
- Gemini Flash or Ollama/Qwen LLM provider.
- Chunked document understanding.
- Parallel specialist AI analysis.
- Evidence validation and final report synthesis.

## Technology stack

| Layer | Technology |
|---|---|
| Frontend | Angular 22 |
| Backend | .NET 10 |
| AI | Gemini Flash / Ollama Qwen |
| Export | Open XML PPTX / QuestPDF |

## Benefits

- Faster review cycles.
- Traceable evidence.
- Consistent severity assessment.
- Exportable executive reports.
